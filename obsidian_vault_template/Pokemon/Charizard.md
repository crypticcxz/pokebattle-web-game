# 🔥 Charizard

Charizard flies around the sky in search of powerful opponents. It breathes fire of such great heat that it melts anything.

## 📊 Base Stats
- **HP**: 78
- **Attack**: 84
- **Defense**: 78
- **Special Attack**: 109 *(Primary offensive stat)*
- **Special Defense**: 85
- **Speed**: 100 *(Tied for second fastest in standard roster)*
- **BST**: 534

## ⚔️ Moveset
- [[Flamethrower]]: Primary STAB Special Fire attack with 10% Burn chance.
- [[Dragon Claw]]: Physical coverage against Dragon types.
- [[Air Slash]]: Secondary STAB Special Flying attack.
- [[Thunder Wave]]: Speed control utility inflicting [[Paralysis]].

## 🧠 Minimax AI Strategy
- In Minimax evaluations, Charizard excels as a fast special sweeper.
- Use [[Thunder Wave]] against faster threats like [[Pikachu]] or [[Gengar]] to gain turn priority.
